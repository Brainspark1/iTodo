# iTodo

iTodo is a minimalistic and customizable to-do list application for Mac, designed to help you stay organized and focused on your tasks. With an emphasis on simplicity and efficiency, iTodo offers a range of powerful features that go beyond traditional task management, making it the perfect tool for both personal and professional use.

## Features

* Task Folders: Organize your tasks into folders to keep related items together. Whether you’re managing a project or planning your week, task folders help you stay on top of everything.
* Password-Encrypted Tasks: Keep your sensitive tasks secure with password encryption. iTodo ensures that your private tasks remain confidential.
* Built-In Timer and Stopwatch: Boost your productivity with integrated timers and stopwatches. Track how long you spend on each task, or set a countdown to stay on schedule.
* Task Editing: Easily modify your tasks as your priorities change. iTodo allows you to update task details, deadlines, and more with just a few clicks.
* Pin Important Tasks: Pin your most important tasks to the top of your list, so they’re always visible and easy to access.
* Completed Tasks Section: Keep track of what you’ve accomplished by sorting tasks into a completed section. Review your progress and stay motivated.

## How to Use

1. Creating Tasks: Add new tasks quickly by clicking the “Add Task” button. You can then assign them to folders, set deadlines, and more.
2. Organizing Tasks: Use task folders to categorize your tasks, and pin essential items to the top of your list for easy access.
3. Using Timers and Stopwatches: Track your time spent on tasks with the built-in timer and stopwatch features. Perfect for managing work sessions or timing specific activities.
4. Securing Sensitive Tasks: Encrypt any task that contains sensitive information by setting a password. Only you will have access to these encrypted tasks.
5. Managing Completed Tasks: Move completed tasks to the dedicated section to keep your workspace tidy. Review this section to track your progress over time.

## Installation

1. Download the latest release of iTodo from the Releases page.
2. Open the downloaded .dmg file and drag the iTodo app to your Applications folder.
3. Launch iTodo from your Applications folder and start organizing your tasks more effectively.
